# Arcology project guidance

For Arcology illustration, scene-art, or visual-direction work, read `docs/arcology-art-direction.md` and inspect `docs/art/reference/mel-atrium-simplified-v2.png` before designing or generating artwork. The author approved this exact image as the standing style on September 13, 2026. Use `docs/art/arcology-illustration-prompt.md` for the reusable prompt and scene variables.

The approved direction is **Arcology Storybook**: deliberately cute, compact people with simple faces, machines constructed from a few rounded forms, confident dark contours, broad colors, and abundant simplified planting. Preserve its shape language as well as its rendering. The outside inspiration and the first, more realistic illustration do not override the approved master.

The author has approved `docs/art/reference/arcology-overview-storybook-v3.png` as the main website landing-page hero and Arcology overview. It supersedes the previous exterior illustration for current local pages. Preserve its flat rooftop park, three-tier subsidiary ziggurats, left-side river and dry right-side countryside. Future points of interest should be mapped onto this approved image.

Read `docs/arcology-reader-experience.md` for character-centered scene navigation, spoiler scope, and hotspot framing. Illustration approval establishes an art direction, not new story canon or engineering facts. Follow the author's current instructions when they revise these decisions.
