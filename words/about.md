## Who {#who}

Ben, host of Life with AI. He builds too. His own build is [Asher](/projects/asher), a small robot car that Claude drives, hears and speaks through.

## What the site is {#what}

Most people meet AI as a chatbot: a box you type into. This site is about the rest of it. It follows AI off the screen and into real work: on the farm, on the job site, at the bench, in the repair bay, in the kitchen, and in the aids people build for themselves.

The site has six topics: [Growing](/topics/growing), [Building](/topics/building), [Making](/topics/making), [Fixing](/topics/fixing), [Feeding](/topics/feeding) and [Living](/topics/living).

## How a piece is made {#how}

Pieces are about real people doing the work, told in their own words. Every piece follows the same plan.

- **Then:** A little history: how this work used to be done.
- **Now:** How most people do it today.
- **Next:** What this builder does differently, with AI and something physical they made.
- **The receipt:** What it cost, and what broke along the way.
- **The core:** For people who want the details: the parts list, the wiring, the AI model used and the power it needs.

## The rules {#rules}

### Sponsors

We only take sponsors whose product Ben has used on his own bench, and we show where. A sponsor never buys a story or a mention. If a sponsor's product appears in a story, that story says so.

### A guest's build

On a guest's build, the links are theirs. We use the guest's own shop or partner links. If they have none, we use plain links and a “Support this builder” link. The site's own paying links go only on Ben's own builds.

## How AI is used here {#ai}

This site and Asher are built with Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. Facts are checked against sources.
