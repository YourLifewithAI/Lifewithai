# Arcology Storybook — reusable illustration prompt

Use with [the approved style reference](reference/mel-atrium-simplified-v2.png) and [the full style guide](../arcology-art-direction.md). Attach the image to the generation request. Replace the bracketed fields before submitting; remove fields that do not apply.

## Fixed style direction

```text
STYLE AUTHORITY
The attached mel-atrium-simplified-v2.png is the controlling visual reference for the Arcology Storybook style. Match its approved cuteness, compact proportions, simplified construction, rich garden colors, and clean graphic drawing. Transfer the style to the requested scene; the atrium's particular subjects and composition belong only where the scene brief calls for them.

Draw a warm, colorful, deliberately cute illustrated solarpunk world. Construct people as compact rounded cartoon figures, roughly four to five heads tall as a starting guide, with varied builds, short torsos, soft shoulders, simple limbs, broad hands, and blunt shoes. Preserve adult identity where appropriate through clothing, context, posture, and sparse age cues. Faces have small dark oval or dot eyes, a small simple nose, and an expressive mouth mark. Hair is a distinctive large silhouette with a few divisions. Clothing has broad shapes and only purposeful folds. Gesture and silhouette carry the person.

Construct robots and devices from two or three dominant simple masses: rounded capsules, broad boxes, short tubes, plain panels, and chunky feet where appropriate. Make function legible through one useful attachment or action. Use only a few graphic surface details. Choose body design, interfaces, and expression according to the character and scene; the reference's seedling robot is one example, not a universal AI body. Preserve AI agency and the story's distinctions between people, embodiments, and tools.

Use confident dark contours with slight hand-drawn irregularity, stronger around foreground silhouettes and sparse inside objects. Build the image from broad color areas with a few clear shadow shapes and restrained texture. Favor fresh yellow-green, deeper leaf green, teal, clear cyan, cream, denim blue, and small orange or gold accents, adapting the balance to the scene's light. Keep skin tones distinct and appropriate to the characters. Let cool shadows and warm light explain space without realistic rendering.

Build lush planting from grouped scalloped foliage, selected broad leaves, simple flowers, and rounded fruit. Draw architecture, pipes, tanks, furniture, animals, background people, and mural figures with the same simplification as the foreground characters. Create abundance through repeated readable forms and ordinary activities. Keep coherent space, clear paths, and quiet areas between discoveries. Let the intended emotion guide expressions and posture; the cute style can hold silence, doubt, grief, or recovery.

Avoid realistic anatomy under cartoon outlines, idealized fashion proportions, finely articulated hands, modeled lips, glossy anime eyes, individual hair strands, intricate robot mechanisms, photographic materials, dense micro-texture, and uniform detail everywhere. No baked-in navigation, titles, labels, signatures, or watermarks unless the scene brief explicitly calls for a particular piece of diegetic text.
```

## Add only when editing an existing scene

```text
EDIT REFERENCE ROLES
The approved mel-atrium-simplified-v2.png supplies the STYLE.
[Existing scene filename] supplies the EDIT TARGET and COMPOSITION.
Preserve [specific framing, landmarks, poses or identifiers]. Change [specific requested content]. Rebuild any realistic human anatomy and mechanically elaborate robots using the approved simplified forms. If style and target disagree on rendering or proportions, follow the approved style reference.
```

If the approved master itself is the edit target, attach it once and state that it supplies both style and composition. An existing character reference may supply identity without supplying style; label that role explicitly too.
