## Art briefs

Overview: a vast mile-high, broadly based stepped Arcology in Texas hill country, approximately ten major planted tiers and hundreds of fine floor lines. A central crown, extensive terraced gardens, atmospheric scale, ink-and-gouache architectural character, warm natural light. The complete structure belongs to one architectural family. No typography, interface, or labels in the artwork. These proportions are a visual brief, not an engineering claim.

Introductory atrium: an eye-level, sunlit, lived-in garden with Mel tending marigolds at a large communal mural; a small utility robot, a corridor speaker through which Pell participates, visible growing systems and water, and neighbors going about their lives. The extraordinary scale is revealed through the windows. Human and AI presence should feel ordinary and specific.

After Water: preserve the introductory atrium's camera and architectural landmarks. Show repair, replanted beds, shared painting, and embodied AI participation near Mel. The mural incorporates a high-water mark as a sunrise. Keep the mood one of care and ongoing work; do not imply the story's consequences have disappeared.
