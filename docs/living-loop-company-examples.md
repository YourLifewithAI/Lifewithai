# The Living Loop — companies behind the concepts

Reviewed 13 September 2026. Prepared for Life with AI’s Arcology farm experience.

Ten company examples cover six rooms: growing plants, raising fish, caring for water and recovering nutrients, cultivating mushrooms, rearing insects, and returning compost to soil. They demonstrate parts of the proposed system at different levels of maturity. None establishes the complete Arcology cycle, its capacity, or the illustrated robot bodies.

AI predicts or guides decisions; robotics moves or handles things; conventional automation controls equipment. Ostara is included as supporting nutrient-recovery engineering, with no AI claim. Commercial use below means the cited source reports an installation or operation, not that we independently audited it. Pilot results, planned expansion and product offerings retain their own labels.

The website links each room to these examples. Company names open official sites; deployment notes link to named customers, technical descriptions or research partners. No supplier has been contacted and no partnership or endorsement is implied.

## Plants

### [Source.ag](https://www.source.ag/)

**AI · Process automation — Grower use reported.**

Models help growers plan cultivation, forecast harvests and control irrigation.

Source describes Rainbow Growers Middenmeer using its irrigation control with peppers, including the growers’ experience of the pilot.

**Fit and limits:** A greenhouse comparison. The case describes pilot experience and grower oversight; it does not validate our mixed crops or promise better flavor or nutrition.

- [Rainbow Growers’ irrigation experience](https://www.source.ag/cases/working-towards-the-future-of-irrigation-management)
- [Current solutions](https://www.source.ag/)

### [Corvus Drones](https://www.corvusdrones.com/en/)

**Robotics · Image analysis — Commercial use reported.**

Autonomous drones observe greenhouse crops and count germination.

Corvus names Plantenkwekerij Gitzels as a user monitoring potato seedlings.

**Fit and limits:** Supports indoor crop scouting. Images cannot establish flavor or nutritional composition.

- [Gitzels example and drone capabilities](https://www.corvusdrones.com/en/)

## Fish

### [ReelData](https://www.reeldata.ai/reelappetite)

**AI · Process automation — Commercial trial; expansion contracted.**

ReelAppetite detects uneaten pellets in tank outflows and adjusts connected feeders.

An October 2024 announcement describes over a year of use at Samherji’s Staður charr site, followed by an agreement covering three sites. The agreement alone does not confirm every installation was completed.

**Fit and limits:** Relevant to land-based tanks. This is camera-and-feeder automation, not the illustrated floating robot; performance must be checked for our species and water conditions.

- [Samherji trial and expansion agreement · 2024](https://www.reeldata.ai/news/samherji-and-reeldata-announce-expansion-agreement)
- [How ReelAppetite works](https://www.reeldata.ai/reelappetite)

### [Aquaai](https://www.aquaai.com/)

**AI · Robotics — Platform offered; deployment unverified.**

Fishlike robots carry cameras and sensors for water monitoring.

Aquaai offers service or demonstration access and describes autonomous navigation and computer vision.

**Fit and limits:** Underwater fishlike robots, not surface skimmers. No named farm installation verified here.

- [Platform and service description](https://www.aquaai.com/)

## Water care

### [Gradiant / Turing](https://gradiant.com/technologies/smartops-ai/)

**AI · Operator decision support — Commercial use reported.**

SmartOps uses operating data to model water-treatment performance and recommend adjustments.

Gradiant’s ENGIE case describes machine-learning recommendations at a Middle Eastern seawater desalination plant, online in 2021. Operators entered the recommended settings.

**Fit and limits:** Evidence for AI assisting water-plant operations. Desalination is a different process from aquaponic biofiltration or safe nutrient transfer; no savings percentage is carried into our design.

- [ENGIE desalination case](https://gradiant.com/success-stories/smartops-ai-desal-optimization/)
- [SmartOps technology](https://gradiant.com/technologies/smartops-ai/)

### [Ostara](https://ostara.com/nutrient-recovery/nutrient-recovery-solutions/)

**Nutrient-recovery engineering — Commercial installation documented.**

Pearl reactors recover phosphorus in struvite fertilizer granules from suitable wastewater streams.

Ostara and Chicago’s MWRD opened a nutrient-recovery facility at the Stickney plant in May 2016.

**Fit and limits:** A supporting recovery process, not an AI or robotics example. Municipal wastewater differs from fish solids; this does not establish direct reuse in hydroponics or recovery of every nutrient.

- [Stickney facility opening · 2016](https://ostara.com/metropolitan-water-reclamation-district-greater-chicago/)
- [Pearl process brochure · Xylem / Evoqua](https://www.xylem.com/siteassets/catalog-assets/documents/products/folder-0002/mu-ostarapearl-br-0222-lores.pdf)

## Mushrooms

### [4AG Robotics](https://4ag.ai/robots-picking-mushrooms-fourth-generation-2025/)

**Robotics — Commercial use reported.**

Robots pick, trim and pack mushrooms in existing growing racks.

In July 2025, 4AG reported live systems in Canada, Ireland and Australia. Planned deployments elsewhere are not counted as completed.

**Fit and limits:** The documented crop is Agaricus. Our oyster mushrooms, ceiling mounts and receiving quad are adaptations requiring development.

- [Commercial deployment report · July 2025](https://4ag.ai/4ag-robotics-raises-40m-series-b-to-power-the-future-of-autonomous-mushroom-farming/)
- [Agaricus harvesting demonstration · January 2025](https://4ag.ai/robots-picking-mushrooms-fourth-generation-2025/)

## Insects

### [Protix](https://protix.com/)

**AI · Robotics — Commercial production; technology company-reported.**

Black soldier fly production converts selected industrial organic side streams into feed and farming ingredients.

Protix describes commercial production and a Dutch blueprint plant opened in 2019. Its operations page identifies AI and robotics among the technologies it uses.

**Fit and limits:** Protix explicitly reports using AI and robotics, but the reviewed pages give limited detail on their individual tasks or measured contribution. Suitable feedstocks and fish-diet formulation remain separate questions.

- [Commercial production and material route](https://protix.com/)
- [Operations and technology description](https://protix.com/careers/operators-and-mechanics)

## Compost

### [Engineered Compost Systems](https://compostsystems.com/)

**Process automation — Commercial use reported.**

Sensors and controls regulate aeration, surface irrigation and process records during composting.

ECS describes its CompTroller installation at Napa Recycling and Waste Services, processing yard and food waste. The case includes finished compost supplied to agriculture and community gardens.

**Fit and limits:** Conventional process automation, not a verified AI system. It supports managed composting and batch records, not our bucket-carrying quad or a promise of instant mature compost.

- [Napa composting installation and return to growers](https://compostsystems.com/case-studies/nwrs-composting/)
- [Enclosed composting comparison · Ishpeming](https://compostsystems.com/case-studies/ishpeming-wwtp-composting/)

### [Sonnenerde](https://www.sonnenerde.at/en/)

**Robotics — Prototype tested in an industrial setting.**

A compost producer helped develop autonomous electric compost turning with Pusch & Schinnerl and university partners.

The ANDREA project names Sonnenerde as coordinator. The researchers’ 2024 report describes an autonomous turner tested in three industrial-site scenarios.

**Fit and limits:** A field-tested prototype, not a verified commercial robot fleet. Outdoor satellite navigation and windrows differ from an indoor compost bay; autonomous control does not by itself establish machine learning.

- [ANDREA project and company partners](https://austria-in-space.at/en/projects/2020/andrea.php)
- [Researchers’ prototype report · 2024 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.13493)

## How this should influence the Arcology design

The strongest references suggest specialized equipment around a shared material-handling system: greenhouse scouts, instrumented fish feeding, rack-based harvesting and controlled composting. Our inference is to keep those modules independently operable, exchange identified batches, and use mobile bodies for tasks where mobility actually helps. These companies have not demonstrated that architecture together.

Evaluate edible yield and consistency alongside taste, nutrition, shelf life, fish welfare, energy and water consumption. The evidence here establishes plausible tools and processes; it does not substantiate universal improvements in product quality or closed-cycle self-sufficiency.

## Source access and boundaries

Official company pages, their installation accounts, product documentation and the ANDREA project record were reviewed. The autonomous compost-turner preprint abstract was available; the later peer-reviewed article (DOI 10.3390/su16156347) was identified but its full text could not be retrieved, so no additional results were inferred. Aquaai’s current offering is documented, but a named deployed farm installation was not verified. Protix’s AI/robotics description is explicit but broad; its precise task allocation and independent performance remain unverified.

No exact performance percentages from marketing pages were promoted to Arcology specifications. Dates describe the linked evidence; a historic installation is not confirmation of uninterrupted operation today. New buildings or expansion plans are not counted as completed deployments.
